FAQs

What is BookSnack?

BookSnack turns books into bite-sized summaries you can read or listen to in a few minutes. Scroll up for the next book, swipe sideways to read its key ideas.

How does the free trial work?

You get full access for 3 days. We remind you a day before it ends, and you can cancel anytime from Manage Subscription without being charged.

Can I listen instead of reading?

Yes. Tap the speaker button on any book in your feed, or "Listen" on a book page, to hear the summary.

How do highlights work?

Tap any summary text in your feed to highlight it. Find all of them under Profile → My Highlights.

Can I read offline?

Tap the download button on a book in your feed. Downloaded books appear in My Library → Downloaded.

How do I change my favourite genres?

Go to Profile → Reading Preference and tap Edit next to Genres or Reading vibe.

How do I contact support?

Email us at support@booksnack.app and we usually reply within one business day.
